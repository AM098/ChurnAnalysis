# ChurnAnalysis
OTT Churn Analytics
The OTT Churn Analytics project analyzes customer subscription data to understand why customers cancel and how businesses can improve retention and revenue.
1. Churn & Revenue Impact Analysis
2. Customer Risk Scoring & Segmentation
3. Support Intelligence & Cancellation Analysis
Tools & Skills Used
- SQL: Data extraction, JOINs, aggregations, and KPI calculations.
- Excel: Data cleaning, analysis, and reporting.
- Data Analytics: Customer segmentation, churn analysis, revenue analysis, and business insights.
